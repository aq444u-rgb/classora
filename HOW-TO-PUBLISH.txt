CLASSORA - HOW TO PUBLISH, INSTALL AND TEACH LIVE
==================================================

What is in this folder
  index.html            the Classora whiteboard (the app itself)
  manifest.webmanifest  lets phones and laptops install it
  sw.js                 makes it work offline after the first visit
  icons/                the app icon
  vendor/               PDF reader + live-class library (work offline)
  _headers              Netlify settings so updates reach teachers

Upload the WHOLE folder (not only index.html).

PUBLISH (free) - pick one
  Netlify: https://app.netlify.com/drop  -> drag this folder -> you get a link.
  GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages also work (upload everything, keep folders).

INSTALL
  Laptop (Chrome/Edge): open your link, click "Install app" in the toolbar.
  Android (Chrome): tap "Install app".   iPhone (Safari): Share > Add to Home Screen.

TEACH LIVE (new)
  1. Open Classora, click the red Live icon in the toolbar, then "Start live class".
     Allow the microphone when asked.
  2. Send the link (Copy link or WhatsApp). Students open it on their phone,
     type their name and tap Join. Installing is optional for them.
     STUDENT APP: students can install "Classora Class" from  yourlink/student/
     (green icon). They open it, type the 6-letter class code and their name.
  3. Students see your board live, hear your voice, can chat and raise a hand.
     When a hand is raised, click "Allow mic" next to the name; the student can
     then speak and everyone hears them. Click "Mute" to take the mic back.
  4. Click "End class" when finished. Use Record board to save the lesson as a video.
  Tips: use earphones (stops echo), keep the board writing large (Phone mode),
  test with 2-3 students before a full class. The class works while your laptop
  and the students' phones have internet. Everything is direct between devices,
  so a very large class (30+) may be slow on a weak laptop connection.

PUBLISHING AN UPDATE
  Replace index.html, change "classora-v4" to "classora-v5" in sw.js, upload again.
